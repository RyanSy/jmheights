# **H2: What To Have Ready When You Call**

## **For a Service Call**

The property address, equipment type, approximate equipment age, symptoms, and any error codes help dispatch the correct technician with the right parts and tools.

Example:\
“Gas furnace in basement. About 12 years old. Blower runs but there’s no flame.”

## **For Installation or Replacement Quotes**

Approximate square footage, equipment age, fuel type, and any comfort or utility-cost concerns.

Major equipment quotes are performed on-site after proper sizing and inspection. JM Heights does not quote major replacements blindly over the phone.

## **For Comfort Club Enrollment**

The property address, heating and cooling equipment list, and approximate ages of the systems.

A technician can also perform a first-visit equipment inventory if needed.

## **For Commercial Properties**

Building type, occupancy type, approximate square footage, equipment summary, and the on-site contact person.

Most commercial projects begin with a property walkthrough before quoting.
