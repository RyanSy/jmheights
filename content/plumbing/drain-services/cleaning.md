# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Single Fixture vs Multiple Fixture Backups**

If only one drain is slow, the problem is usually isolated to that fixture’s branch drain. If multiple fixtures back up at once, the issue may involve the main sewer line instead.

## **Stop Running Water on Fully Clogged Drains**

Continuing to run water into a blocked drain can worsen flooding and increase pressure within the line.

## **Avoid Chemical Drain Cleaners**

Chemical drain cleaners can damage older galvanized and cast iron piping commonly found in older North Jersey homes and often fail to fully solve the underlying blockage.

## **Hair & Grease Cause Many Drain Problems**

Simple preventative habits such as using drain strainers and properly disposing of grease can significantly reduce future drain clogs.

## **Recurring Drain Problems Often Indicate Bigger Issues**

If the same drain repeatedly slows down after cleaning, the issue may involve improper slope, venting problems, or damaged piping rather than simple buildup.
