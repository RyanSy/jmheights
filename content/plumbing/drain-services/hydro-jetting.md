# **Our Hydro Jetting Services**

## **Camera Inspection Before Hydro Jetting**

Every hydro jetting service begins with a sewer camera inspection to evaluate the condition of the pipe and determine whether hydro jetting is the right solution.

We inspect for:

- Cracks or pipe damage

- Root intrusion

- Pipe corrosion

- Collapsed sections

- Offset joints

- Pipe material and condition

Hydro jetting should only be performed on lines that can safely handle the cleaning pressure.

## **Residential Hydro Jetting**

Residential hydro jetting is ideal for:

- Kitchen drain grease buildup

- Recurring sewer clogs

- Sludge accumulation

- Scale buildup

- Minor root intrusion

Pressure settings are adjusted based on the pipe material and condition to safely clean older and newer plumbing systems.

## **Commercial Hydro Jetting**

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Hydro Jetting Is Best for Grease, Sludge & Roots**

Hydro jetting is especially effective for:

- Grease buildup

- Root intrusion

- Mineral scale

- Recurring drain problems

- Heavy sludge accumulation

Simple paper or soft clogs may only require standard drain snaking.

## **Older Pipes Require Proper Pressure Control**

Many older Bergen and Passaic county homes still contain cast iron or clay piping that requires careful pressure calibration during hydro jetting.

## **Recurring Backups Often Indicate Pipe Damage**

If the same drain repeatedly clogs even after cleaning, the issue may involve damaged or deteriorated piping rather than buildup alone.

## **Restaurants Benefit from Preventative Jetting**

Commercial kitchens and grease-heavy businesses often benefit from scheduled hydro jetting maintenance to reduce emergency backups and extend drain system life.

## **Most Hydro Jetting Appointments Include Setup & Inspection Time**

Hydro jetting projects typically include:

- Initial camera inspection

- Equipment setup

- Cleaning process

- Final camera verification
