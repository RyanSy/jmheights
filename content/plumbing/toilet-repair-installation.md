# **Toilet FAQs**

## **Why does my toilet keep running?**

Most running toilets are caused by a worn flapper, faulty fill valve, or improperly adjusted float. Even a slow-running toilet can waste thousands of gallons of water over time.

## **Should I repair or replace my toilet?**

If the toilet is under 10 years old and only needs a simple repair, repair is usually the better option. Older toilets with repeated issues, cracks, or inefficient flushing systems are often better candidates for replacement.

## **How long does toilet installation take?**

Most standard toilet replacements take approximately 1 to 2 hours including removal, installation, testing, and cleanup.
