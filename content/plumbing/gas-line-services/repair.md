# **What We Repair**

## **Black Iron Gas Line Repair**

We repair leaking fittings, corroded sections, damaged gas piping, and failed threaded joints on traditional black iron gas systems.

All repairs are completed using gas-rated materials and pressure-tested before the line is returned to service.

## **CSST Gas Line Repair**

CSST (Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing) requires manufacturer-approved fittings and proper bonding to the home's electrical grounding system.

We repair damaged CSST systems, verify bonding compliance, and inspect for safety issues during every repair.

## **Pressure Testing & Leak Verification**

Every repair is pressure-tested before the gas is restored.

We perform code-compliant pressure testing using calibrated gauges to confirm the integrity of the repaired system.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **If You Smell Gas, Leave First**

Leave the building immediately and contact your gas utility provider from outside the property before calling for repair service.

PSE&G Emergency Gas Line: 1-800-880-PSEG (7734)

## **Know Which Appliance Was Running**

A stronger gas smell near a specific appliance can help narrow down the affected branch line or connection.

## **Black Iron & CSST Are Different Systems**

Black iron gas piping and CSST require different repair methods, fittings, and safety checks. Knowing which type you have can help speed up the repair process.

## **Pressure Testing Is Required**

Gas line repairs should always be pressure-tested before the system is placed back into operation.

## **CSST Bonding Matters**

Improperly bonded CSST systems can create additional safety concerns. We inspect bonding during CSST repair calls.

# **Before You Try It Yourself**

## **Safe to Do Yourself**

- Leave the building if you smell gas

- Call the utility provider from outside

- Document which appliance may be involved

- Shut off gas at the meter only if it is safely accessible

## **Stop and Call a Licensed Plumber**

Do not attempt:

- Gas piping repairs

- Gas line modifications

- Fitting replacements

- Pressure testing

- Appliance gas reconnections

Gas line work requires licensed plumbing professionals and code-compliant testing procedures.

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

Gas line repairs in New Jersey must comply with plumbing and mechanical code requirements. Improper gas work can create fire hazards, carbon monoxide risks, insurance issues, and failed inspections during future property sales.
