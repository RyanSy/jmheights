# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Take a Photo of the Gas Meter**

The gas meter rating helps determine whether your current gas supply can handle the additional appliance load.

## **Pool Heaters & Generators Require Large Gas Loads**

Pool heaters and standby generators are among the highest-BTU residential appliances and may require larger gas lines or utility upgrades.

## **Outdoor Gas Piping Requires Proper Materials**

Underground and outdoor gas lines must use approved materials and installation methods based on local code requirements and exposure conditions.

## **Permit Timing Matters**

Most municipalities require permit approval before installation begins. Planning ahead helps avoid delays on larger projects.

## **Appliance Specs Help Speed Up the Quote**

Having the appliance model and BTU information ready helps us size the system correctly during the estimate process.

# **Before You Try It Yourself**

## **Safe to Do Yourself**

- Choose the appliance location

- Gather appliance specifications

- Photograph the gas meter and install area

- Plan appliance placement with your contractor

## **Stop and Call a Licensed Professional**

Do not attempt:

- Gas piping installation

- Gas line modifications

- Pressure testing

- Gas appliance hookups

- Permit applications requiring licensed plumbing work

Gas installations require licensed plumbing professionals and code-compliant testing procedures.

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

Improper gas installations can create serious fire and carbon monoxide risks. Unpermitted gas work can also create problems during home sales, inspections, insurance claims, and future renovations.
