## **Trust the Smell**

Natural gas is intentionally odorized to help homeowners detect leaks quickly. If you think you smell gas, treat it seriously.

## **Do Not Use Electronics or Open Flames**

Do not turn lights on or off, use appliances, start vehicles, or use anything that could create a spark while inside the property.

## **Gas Leaks & Carbon Monoxide Are Different**

A gas leak involves unburned natural gas escaping from piping or appliances. Carbon monoxide is produced by improper combustion from fuel-burning equipment. Both are dangerous and require immediate attention.

## **Keep Repair Documentation**

Pressure test results, permits, and inspection approvals provide important documentation for future inspections, insurance claims, and property sales.

# **Before You Try It Yourself**

## **Safe to Do Yourself**

- Leave the building immediately

- Call PSE&G from outside

- Keep people away from the area

- Document where the smell was strongest if possible

## **Stop and Call a Licensed Professional**

Do not attempt:

- Gas line repairs

- Opening gas piping

- DIY leak testing

- Appliance gas disconnections

- Pressure testing

Gas leak repair requires licensed plumbing professionals and approved testing procedures.
