# **Sewer Service FAQs**

## **How do I know if I need sewer line repair or full replacement?**

A sewer camera inspection determines the condition of the line. Isolated cracks or root intrusion may only require spot repair, while widespread damage or collapsed sections often require full replacement.

## **Is trenchless sewer repair always the best option?**

Not always. Trenchless methods work well in many situations, especially when protecting driveways, landscaping, or sidewalks is important. Traditional excavation may still be the better solution in certain cases.

## **Do older North Jersey homes still have clay or cast iron sewer lines?**

Yes. Many Bergen and Passaic county homes built before the 1970s still use cast iron, clay, or Orangeburg sewer piping, all of which can deteriorate significantly over time.
