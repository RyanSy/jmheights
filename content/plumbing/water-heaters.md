# **Water Heater FAQs**

## **How long does a water heater last in North Jersey?**

Traditional tank water heaters typically last 8 to 12 years. Tankless water heaters can last 15 to 20 years with regular maintenance and descaling. Hard water conditions in North Jersey can shorten lifespan if maintenance is neglected.

## **Should I repair or replace my water heater?**

If the unit is under 7 years old, repair is often the best option. Older units with leaking tanks, recurring failures, or major corrosion are usually more cost-effective to replace. We’ll explain both options honestly before recommending anything.

## **Is tankless better than a traditional water heater?**

Tankless water heaters provide endless hot water, improved efficiency, and longer lifespan, but they require a higher upfront investment and sometimes gas line upgrades. Traditional tanks are less expensive to install and work well for many homes. The right option depends on household size and usage.
