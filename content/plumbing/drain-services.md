# **Drain Service FAQs**

## **When do I need hydro jetting instead of drain snaking?**

Drain snaking opens a path through the blockage to restore flow, while hydro jetting fully cleans the inside of the pipe. Hydro jetting is often recommended for recurring clogs, grease buildup, scale, or root intrusion.

## **Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?**

Hydro jetting is safe for many drain systems when the pipe condition supports it. We inspect the line first and adjust pressure based on the pipe material and condition.

## **How often should drains be professionally cleaned?**

Most residential drains only need professional cleaning when problems occur. Homes with recurring issues or commercial kitchens may benefit from scheduled maintenance cleaning.
