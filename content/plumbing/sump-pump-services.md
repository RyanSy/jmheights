# **Sump Pump FAQs**

## **How long does a sump pump last?**

Most primary residential sump pumps last approximately 7 to 10 years depending on usage, water conditions, and maintenance.

## **Do I need a battery backup sump pump?**

If your home has a finished basement, a history of water intrusion, or frequent power outages during storms, a battery backup system is highly recommended.

## **How often should a sump pump be tested?**

Sump pumps should be tested at least twice per year, ideally before spring rain season and before fall storm season.
