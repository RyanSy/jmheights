# **Garbage Disposal FAQs**

## **My garbage disposal hums but does not spin. Is it broken?**

Usually not. Most humming disposals are jammed internally. After disconnecting power safely, many jams can be cleared using the reset button and hex key slot underneath the unit. If the motor still will not turn, repair or replacement may be needed.

## **How long does a garbage disposal last?**

Most residential garbage disposals last approximately 10 to 15 years depending on usage habits, maintenance, and horsepower size.

## **What size garbage disposal do I need?**

Most homes use:

- 1/2 HP for lighter kitchen use

- 3/4 HP for average family kitchens

- 1 HP for heavier daily cooking and food prep

We recommend the proper size based on household usage during the estimate.

## **What should not go into a garbage disposal?**

Avoid grease, oil, fibrous vegetables, bones, coffee grounds, pasta, rice, eggshells, and non-food items. These materials commonly cause jams and drain clogs.
