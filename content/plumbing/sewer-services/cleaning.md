# **Our Sewer Line Cleaning Services**

## **Mechanical Cabling & Sewer Snaking**

Mechanical sewer cabling is often the first step for clearing blocked sewer mains. Motorized sewer snakes cut through soft blockages, paper buildup, and minor root intrusion to restore drainage quickly.

## **Hydro Jetting**

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the full interior diameter of the sewer pipe. This method is especially effective for:

- Grease buildup

- Heavy sludge

- Root intrusion

- Mineral scale

- Recurring sewer clogs

Hydro jetting not only clears blockages but also helps restore long-term sewer flow.

## **Sewer Camera Verification**

Every sewer cleaning service can include a camera inspection to confirm the blockage has been fully cleared and identify cracks, offsets, roots, or structural damage that may be contributing to recurring backups.

## **Recurring Sewer Backup Diagnosis**

If the same sewer line repeatedly backs up, the issue is often related to pipe damage rather than a simple clog. Camera inspections help determine whether sewer repair or replacement is needed instead of repeated cleaning.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Stop Using Water During a Main Sewer Backup**

Running sinks, toilets, washing machines, or dishwashers during a sewer backup can worsen flooding and sewage overflow inside the home.

## **Understand the Difference Between Cabling & Hydro Jetting**

Cabling creates an opening through the blockage, while hydro jetting thoroughly cleans the interior walls of the pipe. Older sewer lines with grease, roots, or heavy buildup often benefit more from jetting.

## **Recurring Sewer Backups Usually Indicate a Bigger Problem**

If the same drain repeatedly backs up every few months, there is often structural damage, root intrusion, or deterioration somewhere in the sewer line.

## **Avoid Flushable Wipes & Grease Disposal**

Many sewer clogs are caused by wipes, grease, and improper materials entering the sewer system. Even products labeled “flushable” can contribute to blockages over time.

## **Tree Roots Are Common in Older North Jersey Sewer Lines**

Mature trees throughout Bergen and Passaic counties frequently invade older clay and cast iron sewer pipes through small cracks and joints.

# **Before You Attempt a DIY Sewer Cleaning**

## **Safe Steps for Homeowners**

- Stop running water throughout the house

- Identify which fixtures are backing up

- Locate the sewer cleanout if accessible

- Document when the problem started
