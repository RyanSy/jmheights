# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Know the Pipe Material If Possible**

Older North Jersey homes often still contain original cast iron, clay, or Orangeburg sewer piping. These materials naturally deteriorate over time and become more vulnerable to collapse, root intrusion, and recurring backups.

## **Understand When Replacement Makes Sense**

Replacement is typically recommended when the sewer line has:

- Multiple failure points

- Collapsed sections

- Severe root intrusion

- Significant pipe deformation

- Extensive corrosion or deterioration

## **Confirm Property Line Responsibility**

In many North Jersey municipalities, homeowners are responsible for the sewer line from the home to the property line, while the township maintains the connection beyond that point.

## **Trenchless Isn’t Always the Cheapest Option**

Trenchless sewer replacement may cost more per foot initially, but can reduce restoration costs by preserving driveways, landscaping, patios, and sidewalks.

## **Plan for Permits & Scheduling**

Permit approval and inspections may take several business days depending on the municipality and project scope.
