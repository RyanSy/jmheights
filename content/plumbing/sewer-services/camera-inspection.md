# **Common Reasons to Schedule a Sewer Camera Inspection**

## **Recurring Sewer Backups**

If drains repeatedly clog or back up, a camera inspection can identify whether the issue is caused by roots, collapsed pipe, offsets, or deterioration.

## **Before Buying a Home**

Most standard home inspections do not include sewer camera inspections. Older Bergen and Passaic county homes may still contain aging cast iron, clay, or Orangeburg sewer lines that are hidden underground.

## **Before Sewer Repair or Replacement**

Camera inspections help determine whether the sewer line needs cleaning, spot repair, trenchless repair, or full replacement.

## **Identifying Pipe Material**

Camera inspections can help identify whether the sewer line is:

- Cast iron

- Clay

- Orangeburg

- PVC

- ABS

This information helps guide repair and replacement planning.

# **Helpful Tips Before Your Inspection**

## **Locate the Sewer Cleanout If Possible**

The sewer cleanout is often located:

- In the basement near the main exit line

- Along the foundation exterior

- In the yard near the home

## **Save the Inspection Footage**

Keeping a copy of the sewer inspection video can help with future repairs, resale documentation, or insurance questions later.

## **Understand That Sewer Problems Often Start Underground**

Many sewer issues develop gradually over time and are impossible to identify without a camera inspection.

## **Tell Us the Reason for the Inspection**

Pre-purchase inspections, recurring backups, and pre-repair diagnostics each involve slightly different inspection goals and reporting focus.

# **Before You Attempt a DIY Sewer Inspection**

## **Safe Steps for Homeowners**

- Locating the cleanout

- Documenting symptoms and backup patterns

- Gathering property records if available

- Asking previous homeowners about past sewer repairs

## **Leave These Tasks to Licensed Professionals**

- Sewer camera operation

- Locating buried sewer defects

- Sewer diagnosis and interpretation

- Pushing cameras through blocked lines

- Opening backed-up sewer cleanouts
