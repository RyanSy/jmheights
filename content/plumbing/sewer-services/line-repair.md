# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Watch for Early Sewer Warning Signs**

Common sewer line warning signs include:

- Multiple slow drains

- Gurgling sounds from drains or toilets

- Sewer odors indoors or outdoors

- Water backing up at lower fixtures

- Wet or unusually green patches in the yard

## **Know the Age & Material of the Sewer Line**

Many older Bergen and Passaic county homes still use cast iron, clay, or Orangeburg sewer piping, all of which become more vulnerable to cracking and root intrusion with age.

## **Recurring Backups Usually Mean Structural Problems**

If the same drain line repeatedly backs up even after snaking or cleaning, the issue is often damage or deterioration within the pipe itself.

## **Save Sewer Camera Footage**

Camera inspections can help document pipe condition for insurance claims, future repairs, or home sale inspections.

## **Check Insurance Coverage**

Some homeowner policies include optional sewer service line coverage that may help offset repair costs.

# **Before You Attempt a DIY Sewer Repair**

## **Safe Steps for Homeowners**

- Documenting symptoms and affected fixtures

- Locating the sewer cleanout

- Checking municipal records if available

- Contacting your insurance provider about service line coverage

## **Leave These Repairs to Licensed Professionals**

- Sewer snaking beyond accessible cleanouts

- Excavation near underground utilities

- Pipe repair or replacement

- Sewer line lining or coupling repair

- Work requiring permits or inspections

## **Why Professional Sewer Repair Matters**

Sewer repair often involves underground utilities, municipal sewer connections, excavation safety, and permit requirements. Improper repairs can lead to property damage, recurring backups, failed inspections, or utility hazards.
