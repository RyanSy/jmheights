# **Gas Line Service FAQs**

## **What should I do if I smell gas inside my home?**

Leave the building immediately and contact your gas utility provider first. Avoid using switches, phones, appliances, or anything that could create a spark while inside the property.

## **Do gas line installations require permits in New Jersey?**

Yes. Most gas line installations and many gas repairs require permits and inspections under New Jersey plumbing and construction code requirements.

## **Can you install gas lines for generators and outdoor appliances?**

Yes. JM Heights installs gas lines for generators, pool heaters, outdoor kitchens, grills, ranges, dryers, and other gas appliances.
