# **Signs You May Have a Hidden Water Leak**

You may need professional leak detection if you notice:

- Unexplained increases in your water bill

- Water stains on ceilings or walls

- Mold or mildew odors

- Warm spots on floors

- Sounds of running water when fixtures are off

- Soft drywall or warped flooring

- Constantly running water meter

If you notice any of these signs, early leak detection can help prevent larger and more expensive repairs.
