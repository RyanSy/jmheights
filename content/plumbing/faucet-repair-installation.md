# **Faucet FAQs**

## **Why does my faucet keep dripping after I turn it off?**

Most dripping faucets are caused by worn cartridges, seals, O-rings, or washers inside the faucet body. These components wear over time and allow water to pass through even when the handle is fully closed.

## **Should I repair or replace my faucet?**

If the faucet is relatively new and the issue is isolated to a cartridge or seal, repair is usually the best option. Older faucets with corrosion, repeated leaks, poor water pressure, or discontinued parts are often more cost-effective to replace.

## **Can JM Heights supply the faucet?**

Yes. Homeowners can either provide the faucet they want installed or have JM Heights supply and install a new fixture as part of the flat-rate quote.

## **How long does faucet installation take?**

Most standard faucet replacements take approximately 1 to 2 hours including removal, installation, testing, and cleanup. More complex installations or behind-the-wall valve work may take longer.
