# **What’s Included With Tankless Water Heater Installation**

## **Proper Sizing for Peak Demand**

Tankless systems are sized based on gallons-per-minute demand and winter temperature rise, not simply by home size alone.

We calculate how many fixtures may run simultaneously during peak usage, such as:

- Multiple showers

- Dishwasher use

- Laundry appliances

- Kitchen and bathroom sinks

Proper sizing helps prevent temperature fluctuations and loss of hot water under heavy demand.

## **Gas Line Evaluation & Upgrades**

Tankless systems often require significantly more gas supply capacity than standard tank water heaters. We evaluate gas line sizing and meter capacity to ensure the system receives proper fuel supply under full load conditions.

Undersized gas lines are one of the most common causes of tankless performance problems.

## **Safe Venting & Combustion Air**

Modern condensing tankless systems require proper direct vent installation, exhaust routing, and combustion air planning. We install venting systems to current New Jersey code requirements for safe and efficient operation.

## **Condensate Drainage & Electrical Connections**

Condensing tankless units produce acidic condensate that requires proper drainage and neutralization. Installations also include dedicated electrical connections where required.

## **Permits & Township Inspections**

# **Helpful Tips Before Your Estimate**

## **Take Photos of the Existing Setup**

Photos of the current water heater, gas meter, and electrical panel help speed up the estimate process and allow us to evaluate installation requirements more accurately.

## **Think About Peak Hot Water Usage**

Tankless systems are designed around simultaneous demand, not average use. Homes with multiple bathrooms and larger families often benefit most from tankless systems.

## **Hard Water Maintenance Matters**

North Jersey water conditions can lead to mineral buildup inside tankless systems over time. Annual descaling helps maintain efficiency and extend system lifespan.

## **Understand Installation Timing**

Most tankless installations take a full day due to venting, gas line adjustments, drainage, and electrical setup requirements.

## **Ask About Long-Term Savings**

Tankless systems reduce standby energy loss and typically last longer than traditional tank systems, especially in higher-use households.
