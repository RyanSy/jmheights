# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Shut Off Water & Fuel If the Tank Is Leaking**

If water is actively leaking, shutting off the water supply and fuel source can help minimize damage before the technician arrives.

## **Check the Age of the Water Heater**

Most traditional tank water heaters last about 8 to 12 years. Units over 10 years old with active leaks are usually ready for replacement rather than repair.

## **Know the Fuel Type & Tank Size**

Knowing whether the unit is gas or electric and identifying the approximate tank size helps speed up scheduling and replacement planning.

## **Look Carefully at the Leak Source**

Leaks around fittings or discharge pipes may be repairable. Water pooling directly beneath the tank or rust forming along tank seams usually indicates internal tank failure.

## **Ask About Expansion Tanks**

Most North Jersey homes require thermal expansion tanks due to pressure reducing valves or check valves installed at the water meter.

# **Before You Attempt a DIY Replacement**

## **Safe Tasks for Homeowners**

- Turning off water, gas, or electrical power to the unit

- Photographing the rating plate or serial number

- Clearing access to the installation area

- Removing excess water around the failed unit

## **Leave These Tasks to Licensed Professionals**

- Gas line disconnection or installation

- Electrical disconnects and wiring

- Venting modifications

- Heavy tank removal

- New system installation and code compliance
