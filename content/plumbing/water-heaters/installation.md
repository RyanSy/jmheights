# **What’s Included With Water Heater Installation**

## **Proper Water Heater Sizing**

Water heater sizing depends on the number of bathrooms, fixture demand, and peak household usage. A system that is too small runs out of hot water quickly. A system that is too large wastes energy and increases operating costs.

As a general starting point:

- 40-gallon systems work well for many 1 to 2 bedroom homes

- 50-gallon systems are common for 3 bedroom households

- Larger homes with multiple bathrooms often benefit from 75-gallon or tankless systems

The final recommendation depends on actual household demand, not just square footage.

## **Gas, Electric & Conversion Installations**

We install gas and electric water heaters and handle conversions between fuel types when needed. Before installation begins, we evaluate gas line capacity, venting requirements, and electrical panel availability to ensure the new system can operate safely and efficiently.

## **Safe Venting & Combustion Air**

Gas water heaters require proper venting and combustion air to operate safely. We install atmospheric vent, power vent, and direct vent systems according to current New Jersey code requirements.

Improper venting can create serious carbon monoxide risks, especially in enclosed utility spaces and older homes.

## **Permits & Township Inspections**

JM Heights handles required permits and coordinates inspections for water heater installations throughout Bergen and Passaic counties. All work is completed to NJ Uniform Construction Code standards under NJ Plumbing License \#12023.

## **Expansion Tanks & Safety Components**

Most North Jersey homes require thermal expansion tanks due to pressure reducing valves or check valves installed at the water meter. Installations also include proper drain pans, temperature-pressure relief discharge piping, shutoff valves, and other required safety protections.

# **Helpful Tips Before Your Installation Appointment**

## **Know Your Existing Fuel Type**

Gas and electric systems have different installation requirements. Photos of the current unit, gas meter, and electrical panel can help speed up the estimate process.

## **Think About Location Carefully**

The placement of a water heater affects efficiency, venting, maintenance access, and hot water delivery time throughout the home.

## **Plan for Future Household Demand**

If the home is being renovated, expanded, or adding bathrooms, it may make sense to install a larger-capacity system now instead of replacing it again later.

## **Ask About Permit Timing**

Permit approval timelines vary by municipality. Most townships take several business days to process water heater permits before installation can begin.
