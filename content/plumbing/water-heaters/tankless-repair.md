# **Common Tankless Water Heater Repairs**

## **Error Code Diagnosis**

Tankless systems use onboard diagnostics to display error codes related to ignition failure, overheating, flame loss, scale buildup, airflow problems, and other system faults.

We identify the actual cause of the error before replacing parts or recommending repairs.

## **Heat Exchanger Descaling**

North Jersey’s moderately hard water can create mineral buildup inside the heat exchanger over time. Professional descaling restores performance, improves efficiency, and helps prevent recurring error codes and flow issues.

## **Ignition System Repair**

Ignition failures may be caused by ignitors, flame rods, gas pressure problems, combustion airflow restrictions, or burner issues. Proper diagnosis is critical because similar symptoms can have different causes.

## **Flow Sensor & Mixing Valve Repair**

Temperature fluctuations, inconsistent hot water, or weak hot water flow can often be traced to failed sensors, mixing valves, or clogged inlet filters.

## **Condensate Drain & Neutralizer Service**

Condensing tankless systems produce acidic condensate that must drain properly. Blocked condensate lines or failed neutralizers can trigger shutdowns and fault codes.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

## **Write Down the Error Code**

Providing the exact error code displayed on the unit helps speed up diagnosis and scheduling.

## **Check the Inlet Water Filter**

Some tankless systems have removable inlet filters that can become clogged with debris or sediment over time.

## **Understand the Importance of Annual Descaling**

Tankless systems in North Jersey should typically be descaled annually due to local water hardness conditions. Lack of maintenance is one of the most common causes of tankless performance problems.

## **Listen for Repeated Ignition Attempts**

Clicking sounds without ignition can indicate flame rod issues, ignitor problems, or gas supply issues.

## **Try a Simple Power Reset**

Turning power off at the breaker for about 60 seconds may clear temporary system faults. If the code quickly returns, professional service is usually needed.

# **Before You Attempt a DIY Repair**

## **Safe Troubleshooting Steps**

- Reading the displayed error code

- Resetting power at the breaker

- Checking whether gas shutoff valves are open

- Cleaning accessible inlet filters according to the manufacturer instructions

## **Leave These Repairs to Licensed Professionals**

- Gas valve or burner work

- Heat exchanger servicing

- Ignitor or flame rod replacement

- Condensate drain modifications

- Venting repairs

- Descaling without proper equipment
