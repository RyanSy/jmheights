# **Backflow FAQs**

## **Why is annual backflow testing required in New Jersey?**

Annual testing is required under New Jersey DEP regulations and the Safe Drinking Water Act to confirm the device is properly preventing contaminated water from flowing back into the public water supply.

## **Can any plumber perform backflow testing?**

No. Backflow testing must be completed by a certified backflow tester approved through an NJDEP-recognized certification program in addition to holding the proper plumbing licensing.

## **What happens if my backflow preventer fails the test?**

Most failed devices can be repaired by replacing worn internal components such as check valves, springs, or relief valves. After repairs are completed, the device is re-tested and re-certified.

## **Do you submit the compliance paperwork?**

Yes. JM Heights completes and submits all required testing documentation directly to the appropriate water purveyor and provides copies for your records.

## **Do you install new backflow preventers?**

Yes. We install new RPZ, DCVA, and PVB assemblies for residential and commercial systems including irrigation, fire suppression, and boiler feed applications.
