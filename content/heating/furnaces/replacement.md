# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Check the furnace age first.**

The manufacturing date is listed on the rating plate inside the furnace cabinet. Once a furnace passes 15 to 20 years old, recurring repairs usually point toward replacement instead of continued patchwork repairs.

### **Cracked heat exchangers are immediate safety issues.**

A confirmed crack means combustion gases can enter the supply air. The furnace gets shut down immediately and stays off until replacement is completed safely.

### **High-efficiency systems reduce winter fuel costs.**

A modern 96 percent furnace can lower heating bills substantially compared to older 70 to 80 percent systems, especially during long North Jersey heating seasons.

### **Replacing the AC at the same time can save money.**

If the AC system is already 12+ years old, replacing both systems together avoids future labor duplication and ensures airflow compatibility between heating and cooling equipment.

### **Proper permits and registration matter.**

Township permits, inspections, warranty registration, and rebate paperwork protect the homeowner long-term. A replacement done without them becomes a problem during resale and may void equipment coverage.

# **H2: Furnace Replacement FAQs**

### **When should a furnace be replaced instead of repaired?**

Once a furnace passes 15 to 20 years old, repeated repairs, rising gas bills, or major component failures usually make replacement the better financial decision.

### **How do I know if my heat exchanger is cracked?**

Signs can include CO alarms, soot buildup, flame rollout, unusual odors, or visible cracks during inspection. A confirmed crack requires immediate shutdown and replacement.

### **What efficiency furnace should I install?**

Most North Jersey homes benefit from a 96 percent condensing furnace because of fuel savings and rebate eligibility. In some homes, an 80 percent furnace still makes sense depending on venting and budget.

### **Do replacement furnaces qualify for rebates?**

Yes. Many qualifying high-efficiency systems are eligible for NJ Clean Energy rebates. We help file the paperwork as part of the installation.
