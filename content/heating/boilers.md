# **Boiler FAQs**

## **How long does a residential boiler last?**

Cast-iron boilers often last 25 to 35 years with proper maintenance. High-efficiency condensing boilers typically last around 15 to 20 years. Once major components begin failing near the end of that range, replacement often makes more financial sense than repeated repair.

## **Should I replace my cast-iron boiler with a high-efficiency condensing boiler?**

Sometimes. Condensing boilers can be very efficient, but they require proper venting, condensate drainage, and compatible heating distribution. Cast-iron boilers may still be the better fit for some older homes. We evaluate the home before recommending either option.

## **Is my boiler oversized?**

Many older North Jersey boilers are oversized, especially if the home has received insulation, air sealing, or window upgrades since the original installation. Proper sizing helps reduce fuel use and improve comfort.

## **Can I add an indirect water heater when replacing my boiler?**

Yes. An indirect water heater uses the boiler as the heat source and can be an efficient option when replacing the boiler. It may also eliminate the need for a separate gas water heater.
