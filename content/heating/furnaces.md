# **Furnace FAQs**

## **1. How long does a residential furnace last?**

Most standard-efficiency furnaces last roughly 15 to 20 years. High-efficiency condensing furnaces typically last 12 to 18 years depending on maintenance and operating conditions.

## **2. Should I choose an 80% furnace or a 96% condensing furnace?**

High-efficiency furnaces reduce fuel costs and may qualify for rebates, but they require PVC venting and condensate drainage. Standard-efficiency furnaces are simpler and often less expensive to install. The best choice depends on the home layout, venting path, and long-term plans.

## **3. Are two-stage and variable-speed furnaces worth it?**

Two-stage and variable-speed systems provide more even temperatures, quieter operation, and improved efficiency compared to single-stage equipment. They are especially beneficial in larger homes or homes with comfort imbalances.

## **4. Why does furnace sizing matter so much?**

Oversized furnaces short-cycle, wear out faster, create uneven temperatures, and reduce efficiency. Proper sizing keeps run times stable and improves overall comfort and equipment lifespan.
