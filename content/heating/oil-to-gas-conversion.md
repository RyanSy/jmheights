### **Oil Boiler or Furnace Replacement**

The old oil-fired system is removed and replaced with a properly sized natural gas boiler or furnace. Every conversion includes updated load calculations so the new equipment is sized to the actual home, not the outdated system being removed.

### **Gas Piping Installation**

Gas piping is run from the new meter to the heating equipment, sized correctly for appliance load and pressure requirements. All gas piping is pressure-tested and inspected under NJ plumbing code.

### **Oil Tank Removal**

Above-ground tanks are drained, removed, and disposed properly. Underground storage tanks (USTs) require NJ DEP-compliant removal, soil testing, and contamination assessment when necessary. We coordinate the process from start to finish.

# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Most North Jersey homes already have nearby gas service.**

Much of Bergen and Passaic County already sits on natural gas infrastructure. The first step is confirming whether gas is available at the property.

### **Oil tank condition matters.**

Older above-ground and buried oil tanks become liability risks over time. Conversion is often the best moment to eliminate future leak concerns entirely.

### **Conversions usually lower long-term heating costs.**

Natural gas systems generally operate more efficiently and cost less to run than oil systems, especially when paired with modern high-efficiency equipment.

### **Replacement is the right time to resize the system.**

Most older oil systems were oversized decades ago. Modern load calculations often result in smaller, quieter, and more efficient replacement equipment.

### **Rebates and financing can reduce upfront cost.**

High-efficiency gas equipment may qualify for NJ Clean Energy or utility rebates, and financing options are available for qualifying projects.

### **Why this matters in NJ:**

Oil-to-gas conversion combines gas piping, heating equipment installation, combustion setup, permitting, and environmental compliance into one project. Underground oil tanks in particular are regulated under NJ DEP rules and require proper documentation and testing. Done correctly, the conversion improves efficiency, lowers operating costs, and eliminates long-term tank liability. Done incorrectly, it can create safety, permitting, insurance, or environmental problems later.

# **H2: Oil-to-Gas Conversion FAQs**

### **Is natural gas available at my home?**

Most Bergen and Passaic County homes have nearby gas infrastructure, but availability depends on the street and existing utility setup. We help confirm availability during the quote process.

### **How long does an oil-to-gas conversion take?**

Most conversions take several days once utility coordination and permitting are complete. Underground tank removal or major venting modifications can extend the timeline.

### **What happens if the home has a buried oil tank?**

Buried tanks require excavation, tank removal, soil testing, and NJ DEP-compliant documentation. If contamination is found, remediation may also be required.

### **Are rebates available for oil-to-gas conversion?**

Often, yes. NJ Clean Energy and utility rebate programs frequently apply to qualifying high-efficiency gas heating systems. We confirm available programs at the time of quoting and assist with paperwork.
