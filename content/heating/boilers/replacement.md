# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with short tips.\]

## **Check the boiler age and repair history**

The manufacturing date is listed on the rating plate. Once a boiler reaches 20+ years with recurring repairs, replacement usually becomes the more cost-effective path.

## **Fuel efficiency matters long term**

Older boilers often operate near 70% efficiency while modern condensing systems exceed 95% AFUE. The difference shows up every winter on the utility bill.

## **Boiler replacement is the best time to switch from oil to gas**

If natural gas is available at the property, replacement is often the ideal moment to convert from oil heat to gas. Operating costs are typically lower and equipment options expand significantly.

## **Consider replacing the water heater at the same time**

Many homeowners pair a new boiler with an indirect water heater so one high-efficiency combustion system handles both heating and domestic hot water.

## **Permits and warranty registration matter**

A boiler replacement should include township permits, inspections, and manufacturer warranty registration. Skipping those steps creates problems later during resale, insurance claims, or future repairs.
