## **High-Efficiency Condensing Boilers**

Modern condensing boilers operate above 95% AFUE efficiency and use sealed combustion with sidewall PVC venting. Ideal for well-insulated homes, radiant floor systems, and modern hydronic setups. NJ Clean Energy rebates available on qualifying systems.

## **Cast-Iron Standard-Efficiency Boilers**

Traditional cast-iron boilers remain the right fit for many older North Jersey homes, especially steam systems and homes with older cast-iron baseboard heating. Durable, proven, and often more cost-effective in retrofit situations.

## **Steam Boiler Installation**

Single-pipe and two-pipe steam systems require accurate radiator-load sizing, proper near-boiler piping, vent balancing, and correct commissioning. Steam work is a specialized trade and remains an active part of our scope.

## **Heat-Load Calculation and System Sizing**

We do not size a new boiler by simply matching the old equipment. Manual J calculations for hot-water systems and EDR calculations for steam systems ensure the replacement matches the home's real heating demand.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with short tips.\]

## **Match the boiler to the heating system**

Condensing boilers perform best with low return-water temperatures. Radiant floor systems and modern panel radiators are ideal. Older cast-iron baseboard and steam systems may perform better with standard cast-iron boilers.

## **Oversized boilers are extremely common**

Many older North Jersey homes were sized decades ago before insulation, windows, and air sealing improved. The original boiler is often far larger than today's actual heating load.

## **Consider pairing with an indirect water heater**

A boiler paired with an indirect tank can efficiently handle both home heating and domestic hot water from one system. In many homes, this is the most efficient long-term setup.

## **Steam systems require steam-specific experience**

Steam boilers are not just hot-water boilers with different piping. Proper venting, piping pitch, and boiler sizing are all unique to steam heating systems.

## **Plan around venting requirements**

Condensing boilers typically vent through PVC sidewall piping and require condensate drainage. Standard boilers may continue using the existing chimney, though chimney liner upgrades are sometimes required.

Boiler installation combines combustion equipment, gas piping, hydronic water systems, controls, and township permitting. Incorrect installation creates safety risks, efficiency problems, warranty issues, and failed inspections.
