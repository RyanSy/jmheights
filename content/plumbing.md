# **When to Call an Emergency Plumber in North Jersey**

Some plumbing problems can wait for a scheduled appointment. Others need immediate attention to prevent property damage, flooding, or safety risks.

## **Call Immediately If You Notice:**

- Burst, frozen, or actively leaking pipes

- Sewage backing up into drains or tubs

- Sump pump failure during heavy rain

- Gas smell inside or outside the home

- Multiple drains backing up at once

- Water shutoff valve failure

- No hot water during winter conditions

## **Problems That Can Usually Wait for Scheduled Service:**

- A single slow drain

- Dripping faucets

- Running toilets

- Minor leaks you’ve contained temporarily

- Low water pressure at one fixture

If you’re unsure whether your issue is an emergency, call our office. We’ll help you determine the safest next step.

# **Common Plumbing Problems in North Jersey Homes**

After more than 50 years serving Bergen and Passaic counties, we’ve seen how North Jersey weather, infrastructure, and older housing stock create recurring plumbing issues.

## **Frozen & Burst Pipes**

Older homes with exposed or poorly insulated plumbing are especially vulnerable during prolonged winter cold snaps. Frozen pipes often burst once temperatures rise and water pressure returns.

## **Sump Pump Failures During Storms**

Heavy rain and Nor’easters frequently overwhelm older sump pump systems in flood-prone areas of Bergen and Passaic counties. Battery backups and annual testing help prevent basement flooding.

## **Sewer Backups in Older Municipalities**

Many older North Jersey towns still rely on aging clay sewer laterals that are vulnerable to root intrusion, shifting, and collapse. Camera inspections help identify the exact problem before repairs begin.

## **Hard Water Scale Build-Up**

Hard water throughout North Jersey can shorten water heater lifespan, clog fixtures, and reduce plumbing efficiency over time.

## **Aging Gas Lines**

Older steel gas piping in homes built between the 1950s and 1970s can corrode over time and may require repair or replacement for safety and code compliance.
