# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Peak demand matters more than average usage.**

Restaurants, gyms, salons, and multi-family buildings experience concentrated demand periods that determine proper sizing requirements.

### **Recovery rate is just as important as tank size.**

Two 100-gallon systems can perform very differently depending on BTU input and gallons-per-hour recovery.

### **Tankless banks require proper sequencing controls.**

Commercial tankless systems need controllers that balance runtime across all units and prevent premature wear.

### **Commercial temperature requirements are code-sensitive.**

Restaurants and food-service properties must maintain proper fixture temperatures to meet health-department standards while avoiding scalding risk.

### **Annual flushing dramatically extends equipment life.**

Sediment buildup reduces efficiency, recovery speed, and equipment lifespan. Commercial systems benefit significantly from scheduled maintenance.
