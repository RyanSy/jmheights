# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Multi-boiler plants should stage correctly.**

Buildings with multiple boilers should run the lead boiler efficiently before staging secondary units. Improper sequencing increases fuel consumption significantly.

### **Condensate-return systems are common steam failure points.**

Most steam-system problems begin on the condensate side: pumps, traps, return lines, or vents. Preventative inspection catches many failures early.

### **Summer boiler overhauls reduce winter emergencies.**

The best time for heat-exchanger inspection, burner cleaning, and controls upgrades is during the off-season, not during the first January cold snap.

### **Combustion tuning lowers fuel costs.**

Commercial boilers tuned correctly for CO, O2, and stack temperature can improve operating efficiency noticeably over a heating season.

### **Outdoor-reset controls are one of the easiest efficiency upgrades.**

Outdoor-reset programming automatically adjusts boiler water temperature based on outdoor conditions and reduces unnecessary fuel use.
