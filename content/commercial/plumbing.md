# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Preventative maintenance costs less than emergency downtime.**

Routine drain maintenance, grease trap service, valve testing, and water-heater inspections prevent the large emergency repairs that shut businesses down.

### **Know where your shutoff valves are located.**

Every facility should know the location of the main water shutoff, fixture shutoffs, and isolation valves before an emergency happens.

### **Restaurant grease maintenance is critical.**

Skipped grease-trap maintenance leads to backups, health-code issues, and expensive emergency drain calls.

### **Commercial fixture code requirements continue changing.**

ADA compliance, low-flow fixtures, touchless systems, and updated plumbing standards affect most tenant improvements and remodels.

### **Small leaks become large operating costs.**

Running toilets, leaking flush valves, dripping faucets, and hidden supply leaks add significant cost on commercial water bills over time.

# **H2: What Facility Teams Can Handle vs. When to Call Us**

\[BUILD SPEC: Caution-styled panel.\]

### **Reasonable for in-house maintenance staff:**

Clearing simple sink stoppages.\
Cleaning visible strainers.\
Replacing standard flush valves or fill valves if properly trained.\
Documenting leaks or failures with photos before service arrives.\
Shutting water off at accessible fixture valves.

### **Stop and call us:**

Gas piping work.\
Drain-main or sewer-line repairs.\
Backflow testing and certification.\
Water-service repairs past the meter.\
Commercial grease trap replacement or installation.\
Permit-required plumbing work.\
Any leak affecting walls, ceilings, or multiple tenant spaces.

### **Why this matters in NJ:**

Commercial plumbing work falls under New Jersey plumbing code, municipal permitting, and, in many cases, health-department compliance. Unlicensed work can create inspection failures, water-damage liability issues, and insurance problems. Properly documented licensed work protects the property owner and keeps the building compliant.
