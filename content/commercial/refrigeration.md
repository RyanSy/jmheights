# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Move inventory to a working unit immediately.**

When refrigeration fails, product preservation becomes the first priority. Move high-value or temperature-sensitive inventory first while waiting for service.

### **Door gaskets fail quietly.**

Torn or compressed door gaskets force refrigeration systems to run constantly and drive up energy costs. Replacing worn gaskets early prevents larger system strain.

### **Ice machines require regular water filtration.**

Hard water and scale buildup shorten equipment life and reduce ice production significantly. Routine filter replacement extends equipment lifespan.

### **Frozen evaporator coils usually indicate airflow or defrost problems.**

Most frozen-coil issues are caused by failed defrost systems, blocked airflow, or fan problems, not low refrigerant.

### **Keep a documented temperature log during failures.**

Restaurants and food-service operators should log temperatures, timelines, and corrective actions during refrigeration failures for health-department documentation.
