# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Rooftop-unit age matters more than brand.**

Most commercial RTUs reach replacement range between 12 and 18 years depending on operating conditions and maintenance history.

### **Commercial filter schedules are more aggressive.**

Restaurants, salons, fitness studios, and retail spaces usually require monthly to quarterly filter changes due to occupancy and operating load.

### **Economizers quietly waste money when they fail.**

A stuck economizer damper or failed actuator can increase cooling costs significantly without obvious symptoms inside the building.

### **Belt-drive systems require scheduled maintenance.**

Commercial blower belts and bearings wear on a predictable cycle. Preventative replacement avoids expensive emergency downtime.

### **Refrigerant regulations are changing.**

Commercial HVAC equipment is transitioning away from R-410A toward lower-GWP refrigerants like R-454B and R-32. Replacement decisions now involve refrigerant planning as well as equipment age.
