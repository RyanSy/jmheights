# **H2: Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel, 5 short tip blocks.\]

### **Preventive maintenance costs less than emergency response.**

Scheduled maintenance dramatically reduces after-hours failures and emergency repair costs over time.

### **Documentation matters more than most owners realize.**

Tenant agreements, insurance audits, reserve studies, and health inspections all benefit from organized service records.

### **One mechanical contractor simplifies accountability.**

Using separate vendors for HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing, and boilers often creates communication gaps and inconsistent maintenance standards.

### **PM scheduling should fit building operations.**

Restaurant work before opening, office work after hours, and retail maintenance during low-traffic windows reduce operational disruption.

### **Replacement forecasting is part of good maintenance.**

Preventive maintenance should identify aging systems before failure becomes an emergency replacement situation.
