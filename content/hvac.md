# **When to Call an Emergency HVAC Technician**

Some HVAC issues can wait until the next available appointment. Others should be handled immediately.

## **Call Right Away If You Have:**

- No heat during freezing temperatures

- AC failure during a heat advisory

- Gas smell near HVAC equipment

- Carbon monoxide alarm activation

- Water leaking from the AC into ceilings or walls

- Electrical arcing, smoke, or burning smells

- Furnace tripping breakers repeatedly

- Frozen AC coils during high outdoor temperatures

- Boiler leaking or losing pressure continuously

## **Issues That Usually Can Wait:**

- Weak airflow from one vent

- Dirty air filters

- Minor thermostat programming issues

- Slight humidity imbalance

- Gradual noise increase from older equipment

- Slow temperature changes in one room

If you are unsure whether the issue is urgent, call us. We would rather help you make the right decision quickly than leave you without heat or cooling when the weather turns extreme.

# **Common HVAC Problems We See in North Jersey**

## **AC Failures During Summer Heat Waves**

Extended stretches of 90+ degree weather place heavy strain on aging compressors, capacitors, blower motors, and refrigerant systems. Emergency AC repairs spike during these periods throughout Bergen and Passaic Counties.

## **Furnace Short-Cycling in Older Homes**

Many older North Jersey homes were built with undersized or outdated ductwork. Furnaces cycle too quickly, creating uneven temperatures and unnecessary wear on the equipment.

## **Boiler Problems in Pre-1960 Housing**

Older steam and hot water boiler systems remain common in cities like Paterson, Passaic, Hackensack, and Clifton. Once systems pass 25 to 30 years old, replacement often becomes more cost-effective than ongoing repairs.

## **Ductwork Leaks in Attics and Crawlspaces**

Separated joints and aging flex duct waste conditioned air and increase utility costs. We regularly repair and seal duct systems in older Bergen and Passaic homes.

## **Improperly Sized Heat Pumps**

Heat pumps work extremely well in North Jersey when sized and configured correctly. Systems installed too small or with incorrect backup heat settings lose efficiency quickly and increase operating costs.
