# **H2: What To Do Before the Technician Arrives**

## **Plumbing Emergencies**

Shut off the water supply if possible. The main shutoff is usually near the water meter or where the line enters the home.

For water-heater leaks, shut off the cold-water supply and power or gas to the unit.

For overflowing toilets, shut off the fixture valve behind the toilet.

## **Heating Emergencies**

Turn the thermostat off or significantly down and leave the system alone until the technician arrives.

If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call 911 or PSE&G first.

## **Cooling Emergencies**

Turn the AC system off at the thermostat. If the indoor coil is frozen, leave the system off until it thaws.

Use fans and ventilation where safe until service arrives.

## **Sewer Backups**

Stop using all sinks, toilets, showers, and laundry immediately. Every gallon of water makes the backup worse.

Move valuables and belongings off the floor if flooding is occurring.

## **Carbon Monoxide Alarm**

Leave the home immediately and call 911. Do not re-enter until emergency services clear the property.
