# **When to Call an Emergency Heating Technician**

Some heating issues can wait for a scheduled appointment. Others should be handled immediately.

## **Call Right Away If You Have:**

- No heat during winter

- Gas smell in or near the home

- Carbon monoxide alarm activation

- Smoke, soot, or burning smells from heating equipment

- Water leaking from a boiler

- Heating system repeatedly tripping the breaker

- Pilot light or burner repeatedly shutting off

- Vulnerable household members without heat

## **Issues That Usually Can Wait:**

- One room colder than the rest

- Thermostat batteries or programming issues

- Mild humming or rattling that is not new

- Dirty air filter replacement

- Gradual comfort imbalance

If you are unsure whether the issue is urgent, call us. We can help you decide quickly and safely.

# **Common Heating Problems We See in North Jersey**

## **Furnace Short-Cycling During Cold Snaps**

During extended winter cold, aging or undersized furnaces often begin short-cycling, struggling to maintain temperature, or triggering ignition and pressure switch problems.

## **Boiler Problems in Older Homes**

Steam and hot-water boilers are common in Hackensack, Paterson, Passaic, Clifton, and other older North Jersey communities. Common issues include failed circulators, leaking sections, aging gas valves, and pressure problems.

## **Oil-to-Gas Conversions in Pre-War Homes**

Many older homes still rely on oil heat. Converting to natural gas can lower fuel costs, eliminate tank concerns, and open the door to higher-efficiency heating equipment.

## **Heat Pump Performance Issues**

Improperly sized heat pumps, poor backup heat settings, and defrost cycle problems can reduce comfort and increase electric usage during shoulder seasons and cold snaps.

## **Cracked Heat Exchangers in Older Furnaces**

Furnaces over 15 years old should be inspected regularly for heat exchanger cracks, combustion problems, and carbon monoxide risks.
