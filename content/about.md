# **Our Story**

JM Heights began in 1969 as a local heating and cooling company serving North Jersey homeowners. Over the years, the company expanded to include full plumbing, heating, and HVAC services while staying rooted in the same communities we started in.

Three generations later, we still service the same neighborhoods across Bergen and Passaic counties. From older homes in Hackensack and Paterson with steam boilers and cast iron plumbing to modern HVAC systems in newer Paramus and Wayne construction, our team understands the housing stock, infrastructure, and service challenges unique to North Jersey.

We never franchised the business, changed ownership, or outsourced our work. The people answering the phones, dispatching the trucks, and showing up at your home are part of the JM Heights team.

# **What Makes JM Heights Different in North Jersey**

## **On-Staff Mechanical Engineer**

Most residential contractors size heating and cooling systems based on what was already installed or by using rough estimates. JM Heights takes a more precise approach.

Our on-staff mechanical engineer performs real system planning, including Manual J load calculations, ductwork sizing, pipe layout planning, and engineered installation design before equipment is ordered or installed. This helps systems run more efficiently, last longer, and avoid unnecessary strain or premature failure.

It’s a level of planning rarely found in residential service companies of our size in North Jersey.

## **Three Trades Under One Company**

Plumbing, heating, and HVAC work often overlap, especially in older North Jersey homes. A boiler issue can affect the plumbing. A water heater replacement may involve HVAC venting or gas line work.

JM Heights handles all three trades in-house under one company and one licensed team. Nothing is outsourced to unknown subcontractors, and customers don’t need to coordinate multiple contractors for the same issue.
