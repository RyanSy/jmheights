# **The Mechanical Engineer Difference**

A properly designed HVAC or plumbing system starts long before installation day.

JM Heights uses real engineering and load calculation practices to properly size heating and cooling systems based on the structure of the home, insulation levels, airflow requirements, and building layout. Ductwork, pipe routing, and system capacity are planned before equipment is installed.
