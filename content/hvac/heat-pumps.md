# **Heat Pump FAQs**

## **Do heat pumps actually work in New Jersey winters?**

Yes, when the correct equipment is installed. Cold-climate heat pumps maintain strong heating performance even during freezing temperatures common throughout Bergen and Passaic Counties. Equipment selection and proper sizing matter significantly.

## **Should I keep my furnace as backup?**

Sometimes. Many homeowners choose a dual-fuel setup where the heat pump handles most heating and cooling while the furnace serves as backup during extreme cold. Others go fully electric with electric backup heat inside the air handler. We recommend the right setup based on the home and energy goals.

## **How much are NJ heat pump rebates?**

NJ Clean Energy rebate amounts change periodically based on program funding and equipment efficiency requirements. We confirm current incentives at the time of estimate and file the rebate paperwork for qualifying systems.

Financing details →

## **Heat pump or traditional AC and furnace?**

For homes with aging HVAC equipment, homes without natural gas, or homeowners focused on long-term energy efficiency, heat pumps are often the better investment. For newer furnace systems with only an aging AC unit, a traditional replacement may make more sense. We provide honest recommendations based on the house and budget.

## **How long does a heat pump last?**

Most modern heat pumps last 12 to 18 years with proper maintenance. Annual service helps extend system life, maintain efficiency, and protect manufacturer warranty coverage.

## **Do heat pumps lower energy bills?**

In many North Jersey homes, yes. Modern inverter-driven heat pumps use less electricity than older electric systems and can significantly reduce heating costs compared to electric resistance heat or aging HVAC equipment.
