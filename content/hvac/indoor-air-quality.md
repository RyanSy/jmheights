# **Indoor Air Quality FAQs**

## **1. Do whole-home IAQ systems work better than portable units?**

For full-home coverage, yes. Whole-home systems treat all circulated air every time the HVAC system runs. Portable units work well for individual rooms but cannot match full-house coverage.

## **2. Do I really need duct cleaning?**

Not always. Duct cleaning is most useful after renovations, visible dust buildup, rodent or insect activity, water damage, or long HVAC inactivity. We inspect the ductwork first before recommending cleaning.

## **3. What indoor humidity level is best for a North Jersey home?**

Most homes feel best between 30% and 40% indoor humidity during winter. Lower humidity causes dryness and static. Higher humidity increases condensation and mold risk.

## **4. Are UV lights worth adding to the HVAC system?**

UV-C lights help prevent mold and bacterial growth on indoor evaporator coils, especially during humid summers. They work best when combined with proper filtration and airflow management.
