## **Whole-Home Ducted Dehumidifiers**

Integrated into the existing HVAC duct system and controlled by a humidistat. Removes moisture throughout the home independently of whether the AC is running.

## **Basement and Crawl Space Dehumidifiers**

Dedicated systems for below-grade moisture problems, musty finished basements, and humid crawl spaces. Drains directly to a condensate line, floor drain, or pump system.

## **AC and Humidity Load Balancing**

Air conditioning handles temperature. Dehumidifiers handle moisture. Properly balancing the sensible and latent load keeps the house cooler, less sticky, and more energy efficient.

## **Annual Service and Inspection**

Whole-home dehumidifiers require regular filter, coil, and condensate inspections to maintain efficiency and prevent drainage issues. We include service options through HVAC maintenance plans.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with 4 to 5 short tip blocks.\]

## **Healthy Summer Humidity Is Around 45 to 55 Percent**

Humidity above 60 percent creates a muggy feel, increases mold risk, and encourages dust mites and mildew growth. Whole-home dehumidifiers maintain the ideal range automatically.

## **AC Alone Does Not Always Control Humidity**

On mild but humid days, the AC may not run long enough to remove moisture properly. A dehumidifier works independently of cooling demand.

## **Basements Naturally Hold More Moisture**

Cool below-grade walls attract condensation during humid weather. Finished basements often benefit from dedicated dehumidification rather than relying on the upstairs HVAC system.

## **Whole-Home Units Are Different From Portables**

Portable units are loud, handle only one room, and require constant emptying. Whole-home systems drain automatically, operate quietly, and treat the entire space.

## **Crawl Space Moisture Creates Structural Problems**

Excess humidity in crawl spaces contributes to mold, wood rot, odors, and pest activity. Proper sealing and dehumidification help protect the home structure long-term.

# **Before You Try It Yourself**

\[BUILD SPEC: Caution-styled panel.\]

## **Safe to Do Yourself**

- Measure humidity levels with a hygrometer

- Identify damp or musty problem areas

- Photograph the HVAC system or crawl space

- Locate possible drain points

- Document condensation or odor issues

## **Stop and Call Us**

- Cutting into supply or return ductwork

- Wiring humidistats or HVAC controls

- Installing condensate drainage systems

- Connecting dehumidifiers to the HVAC system

- Sealing crawl spaces without moisture planning

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

Whole-home dehumidifier installation affects HVAC airflow, drainage, electrical controls, and long-term moisture management. Incorrect installation can create flooding, restricted airflow, or humidity issues that worsen instead of improve.
