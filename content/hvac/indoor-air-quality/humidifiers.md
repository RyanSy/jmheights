# **What We Do**

\[BUILD SPEC: 4-up grid on desktop, 2x2 on tablet, stacked on mobile.\]

## **Bypass Humidifiers**

Economical whole-home humidifiers mounted directly into the duct system. Uses the furnace blower to circulate humidified air throughout the home. Ideal for most residential forced-air systems.

## **Fan-Powered Humidifiers**

Higher-capacity humidifiers with built-in fans that move air independently through the unit. Useful for larger homes or duct configurations where additional airflow improves humidification performance.

## **Steam Humidifiers**

Electric steam humidifiers designed for larger homes, tightly sealed homes, heat pump systems, or homes without traditional furnace airflow. Produces the highest humidity output of residential humidifier systems.

## **Annual Service and Water Panel Replacement**

Water panels gradually mineralize from normal water use and should be replaced annually. We include humidifier maintenance and water panel replacement as part of seasonal HVAC service plans or standalone visits.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with 4 to 5 short tip blocks.\]

## **Healthy Winter Humidity Is Around 35% to 45%**

Below 30% humidity, homeowners typically notice dry air symptoms. Above 50% humidity during winter can create window condensation and increase mold risk.

## **Different Heating Systems Need Different Humidifiers**

Bypass and fan-powered humidifiers pair well with furnaces and air handlers. Steam humidifiers are often the better solution for heat pumps, boiler-heated homes, or larger homes with higher humidity demand.

## **Hard Water Affects Water Panels**

North Jersey water naturally contains minerals that build up inside humidifier panels over time. Annual replacement keeps the system operating efficiently.

## **Whole-Home Systems Work Differently Than Portable Units**

Portable humidifiers treat one room and require manual refilling. Whole-home humidifiers connect to plumbing and automatically maintain humidity throughout the house.

## **Window Condensation Means Humidity Is Too High**

If windows fog heavily during winter, the humidistat setting is likely too high for outdoor conditions. Modern controls automatically adjust humidity targets based on outside temperature.

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

Whole-home humidifier installation involves HVAC controls, plumbing connections, duct modifications, and sometimes dedicated electrical work. Improper installation can damage HVAC equipment, leak into duct systems, or create moisture issues inside the home.
