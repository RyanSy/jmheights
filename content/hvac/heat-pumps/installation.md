# **What We Do**

\[BUILD SPEC: 4-up grid on desktop, 2x2 on tablet, stacked on mobile.\]

## **Manual J Load Calculation**

Heat pumps are sized to the home's actual heating and cooling load, not just square footage. We perform Manual J calculations on every installation to avoid oversized systems that short-cycle or undersized systems that rely too heavily on backup heat.

## **Cold-Climate Heat Pump Selection**

Modern cold-climate systems maintain strong heating output during freezing weather common throughout Bergen and Passaic Counties. We match the right equipment to the home's insulation, layout, and winter heating demands.

## **Backup Heat Strategy**

Most North Jersey heat pump systems include either electric auxiliary heat or a dual-fuel setup using an existing gas furnace. We configure the changeover point so the heat pump handles as much heating as possible while maintaining comfort and efficiency.

## **Permits and Rebate Coordination**

We pull township permits, complete startup documentation, and file NJ Clean Energy rebate paperwork for qualifying installations. Most homeowners qualify for significant incentives when installing approved high-efficiency systems.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with 4 to 5 short tip blocks.\]

## **Heat Pumps Replace Both Heating and Cooling**

A heat pump handles air conditioning in summer and heating in winter using the same system. For homes replacing both an aging AC and furnace, the long-term math often favors a heat pump installation.

## **Cold-Climate Equipment Matters**

Older heat pumps struggled during freezing temperatures. Modern cold-climate variable-speed systems continue operating efficiently far below freezing, making them practical for New Jersey winters.

## **NJ Rebates Can Offset Installation Costs**

The NJ Clean Energy Program offers rebates for qualifying heat pump systems, with incentive amounts changing based on equipment efficiency and current program funding.

## **Dual-Fuel Systems Are Popular in North Jersey**

Many homeowners keep an existing gas furnace as backup while allowing the heat pump to handle most heating and cooling. This approach balances efficiency and cold-weather performance.

## **Proper Sizing Is More Important Than Square Footage**

Two homes with identical square footage can have completely different heating loads depending on insulation, windows, air leakage, ceiling height, and orientation. Proper calculations prevent expensive comfort problems later.
