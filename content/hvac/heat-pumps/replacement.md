## **Replacement vs Repair Evaluation**

Once a heat pump reaches 12 to 15 years old, major repairs like compressors, reversing valves, or coil failures often push the economics toward replacement. We walk through both paths honestly so you can make the right long-term decision.

## **R-22 Refrigerant System Replacement**

R-22 refrigerant production ended in 2020. Older R-22 systems are increasingly expensive to repair and recharge due to shrinking refrigerant availability. We replace outdated systems with modern R-410A, R-454B, or R-32 equipment.

## **Cold-Climate Heat Pump Upgrades**

Modern inverter-driven cold-climate systems maintain heating capacity through freezing temperatures far better than older baseline heat pumps. Most homeowners see improved comfort, lower auxiliary heat usage, and quieter operation after upgrading.

## **NJ Clean Energy Rebate Coordination**

Qualifying replacement systems may be eligible for NJ Clean Energy Program incentives. We handle the paperwork submission as part of the installation process.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with 4 to 5 short tip blocks.\]

## **Check the Refrigerant Type on Your Existing System**

The outdoor unit rating plate lists the refrigerant type. Older R-22 systems that require refrigerant work are often replacement candidates because of the cost and availability of R-22.

## **Major Repairs on Older Systems Usually Trigger Replacement Conversations**

Compressors, reversing valves, indoor coils, and major refrigerant leaks become harder to justify financially once the system reaches 12 years or older.

## **Replacement Is the Right Time to Correct Sizing Issues**

Many existing systems are oversized or undersized for the home. We perform a fresh Manual J load calculation rather than automatically matching the old system tonnage.

## **Variable-Speed Systems Improve Comfort**

Inverter-driven systems operate quieter, dehumidify better, and maintain steadier temperatures than older single-stage systems. We explain the pros and trade-offs during the estimate process.

## **The Indoor Unit Usually Needs Replacement Too**

A new outdoor heat pump should be paired with a compatible indoor air handler. Mixing new outdoor equipment with aging indoor components often creates efficiency, compatibility, and warranty issues.

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

Heat pump replacement requires EPA-certified refrigerant handling, township permits, startup testing, and manufacturer registration. Unlicensed or unpermitted replacement work can void warranties, disqualify rebates, and create problems during inspections or home sales.
