## **Repair Versus Replacement Analysis**

Once a system reaches 12 to 15 years old, major repairs often stop making financial sense. We evaluate repair cost, refrigerant type, efficiency loss, and expected lifespan honestly before recommending replacement.

## **R-22 System Replacement**

R-22 refrigerant was phased out in 2020 and continues to become more expensive and difficult to source. Older R-22 systems are common replacement candidates throughout North Jersey.

## **SEER2 Efficiency Upgrades**

Modern SEER2 systems operate significantly more efficiently than older equipment, lowering cooling costs while improving comfort and humidity control. Many qualifying systems also qualify for NJ Clean Energy rebates.

## **Full System Replacement**

The condenser, evaporator coil, and air handler or furnace are replaced together as a matched system to preserve warranty coverage and maximize performance.

# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with 4 to 5 short tip blocks.\]

## **Check the Refrigerant Type**

The outdoor condenser rating plate lists the refrigerant type. Systems using R-22 are often no longer economical to repair if refrigerant-related work is required.

## **Recalculate the Cooling Load**

Replacement is the opportunity to correct sizing problems from the original installation. A fresh Manual J load calculation helps prevent another oversized or undersized system.

## **Higher-Efficiency Systems Improve Humidity Control**

Two-stage and variable-speed systems run longer at lower output, maintaining more even temperatures and better dehumidification throughout humid North Jersey summers.

## **Refrigerant Line Sets Matter**

Older line sets may contain incompatible oils from previous refrigerants. We either replace or properly flush the line set depending on system requirements.

## **Ductwork Affects System Performance**

Leaking or undersized ducts reduce efficiency and comfort no matter how good the equipment is. Duct condition is assessed during replacement quotes.

# **Before You Try It Yourself**

\[BUILD SPEC: Caution-styled panel.\]

## **Safe to Do Yourself**

- Photograph the existing equipment and rating plates

- Gather utility bills for efficiency analysis

- Document comfort issues and cooling patterns

- Research SEER2 efficiency tiers and rebates

- Photograph ductwork and electrical panels if accessible

## **Stop and Call Us**

- Refrigerant recovery or handling

- Disconnecting or replacing condensers

- Electrical work and disconnect installation

- Duct system modifications

- Permit applications requiring HVAC licensing

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

AC replacement requires EPA-certified refrigerant handling, township permits, licensed HVAC installation, and manufacturer registration. Proper installation preserves warranty eligibility, rebate qualification, and long-term system performance.
