# **Helpful Tips Before You Call**

\[BUILD SPEC: Light-background panel with 4 to 5 short tip blocks.\]

## **Schedule Maintenance Before Peak Summer**

March and April appointments are easier to schedule and reduce the chance of discovering problems during July heat waves when emergency demand spikes.

## **Replace the Filter Regularly**

Most residential systems using a 1-inch filter should have it replaced every 2 to 3 months. Pet owners and high-traffic households may need more frequent changes.

## **Keep the Outdoor Unit Clear**

Maintain at least two feet of clearance around the condenser. Leaves, bushes, fencing, and debris reduce airflow and lower efficiency.

## **Watch for Early Warning Signs**

Longer cooling cycles, rising electric bills, water around the indoor unit, or unusual noises are often early indicators of developing AC problems.

## **Maintenance Plans Usually Pay for Themselves**

Priority scheduling, discounted repairs, and waived diagnostic fees often offset the annual membership cost after a single repair visit.

# **Before You Try It Yourself**

\[BUILD SPEC: Caution-styled panel.\]

## **Safe to Do Yourself**

- Replace air filters regularly

- Rinse the outdoor condenser gently with a hose

- Keep debris away from the outdoor unit

- Verify thermostat settings

- Keep the indoor equipment area clean and unobstructed

## **Stop and Call Us**

- Refrigerant handling or charging

- Electrical work inside the condenser or air handler

- Capacitor or contactor replacement

- Deep evaporator coil cleaning

- Any work involving HVAC control boards or motors

## **Why This Matters in New Jersey**

Manufacturer warranties typically require documented annual maintenance performed by a licensed HVAC contractor. Skipping routine service can void warranty coverage on future repairs.
